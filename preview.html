Everything You Need
to Know.

Do you have MOQs?
+
Absolutely not. No minimum order quantities — ever. Whether you're sourcing one bespoke piece for a residential client or outfitting an entire hotel, we accommodate every scale. This is one of our core promises to design professionals.
How long will my order take?
+
Standard production lead times are 3–6 weeks depending on the product type, customisation and quantity. Samples can be dispatched within 7–10 days. We'll confirm exact timelines in your personalised quote.
Are you open to third-party inspections?
+
Always. We operate an open-door factory policy and actively welcome third-party quality inspections at any stage of production. Transparency is not a policy for us — it's how we operate every day.
What materials do you use?
+
We work with a curated range of premium natural and recycled materials — New Zealand wool, cotton, jute, denim and viscose. Every material is selected for its quality, provenance and environmental footprint. We can also custom-source for bespoke projects.
Can I get custom designs for my project?
+
Yes — and this is where we shine. Bring us your mood boards, colour palettes and specifications and our design team will work directly with the artisans to create bespoke pieces. Custom colorways, sizes, patterns and techniques are all possible.
What makes The Owl Artisans different?
+
We were built specifically for design professionals. No MOQs, direct factory pricing, in-house photography, content creation, and a team that genuinely cares about your project's success. We're not a catalogue — we're a creative partner with a conscience.
